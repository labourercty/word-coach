Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Word Coach ("the game", "we") handles information. Contact: sup6portteam.7@gmail.com

Information we store

The game has no accounts and no game server. Your progress (coins, levels, settings, daily rewards) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and device and app information to show and measure ads, and to personalise them where allowed. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK) you will be asked for consent. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Fonts

The game may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive your IP address when it does so.

Purchases

If in-app purchases or subscriptions are offered, they are processed by the store or payment provider you use. We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
